## How long does setup take?

About an hour at your property. We enter your rooms, rates and photos, paste your calendar links into Airbnb and Booking.com, connect your WhatsApp number and run a small test deposit. Your site goes live at your-name.staydirect.lk the same day. Approval of a new PayHere merchant account can take several working days, so we can start with bank transfer and LankaQR while you wait.

## Do I have to leave Booking.com or Airbnb?

No. Keep them for guests who have never heard of you. StayDirect keeps your calendars in step with theirs, and helps you move repeat and referral guests, and people who find you on Google, to your own site, where you pay a small payment fee instead of OTA commission.

## Who holds the guest's money?

You do. PayHere, PayPal and LankaQR are connected to your own merchant and bank accounts, so deposits and balances go straight to you. StayDirect never holds guest funds and never sees card details, which guests enter on the payment provider's own page. Your StayDirect subscription is billed to you separately.

## Which payment methods can my guests use?

Cards, mobile wallets and internet banking through PayHere; PayPal for international guests; LankaQR, which Indian guests can scan with a UPI app; bank transfer with a slip upload that your manager confirms; and cash at check-in, marked paid in the app. You choose which methods appear on your site and whether you price in rupees or US dollars.

## What do the payment providers charge?

PayHere publishes its fees: [3.30% on Lite with no monthly fee, 2.99% on Plus at LKR 3,990 a month](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/), plus 1.0% when a guest pays in a foreign currency. The merchant fee on [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 has been waived since April 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306). PayPal and your bank set their own fees.

## How does calendar sync with Airbnb and Booking.com work?

Each room gets a calendar link that you paste into Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda or Vrbo, and each room can read up to five calendar links from them. StayDirect checks those links every 15 minutes, with a "sync now" button for busy days. Calendar links carry dates only, not guest names, and the app shows when each room last synced successfully.

## Can two channels still book the same night?

StayDirect itself will never sell a night twice: the database refuses a second booking for the same room and night. The OTAs read your StayDirect calendar on their own schedule, so a clash is still possible in a short gap. If one happens, you get a WhatsApp alert with both booking references and a suggested next step.

## How does WhatsApp work with StayDirect?

Connect your existing WhatsApp Business number or a new one, and guest messages arrive in an inbox in the owner app. Quick replies cover the questions guests ask most, such as directions, check-in, Wi-Fi, breakfast and transport, filled in from your property details. Confirmations, directions and pre-arrival messages go out on WhatsApp and email automatically.

## Which languages are supported?

Guest sites, confirmations and messages run in English, German, French, Hindi and Sinhala, and the guest can switch language on your site. The owner app and owner web run in English and Sinhala, so a manager or cleaner can work in the language they read best. You can also write your own quick replies in any language.

## How does the percentage plan work?

Instead of a monthly fee, you pay a share of confirmed direct bookings: 2.5% on Basic and Pro, 2.0% on Pro Multi and 1.5% on Manager. A month with no direct bookings costs nothing. Bookings made through Airbnb or Booking.com are not charged. The amount is worked out from confirmed bookings in the system, and bookings can be cancelled with a reason but not deleted.

## Can I use my own domain name?

Yes, at no extra cost. Every property starts on a free address such as your-name.staydirect.lk. If you own a domain, you add one DNS record with your domain provider, we set up the secure certificate, and your booking site then runs on your own address. We can help with the DNS step during setup.

## Will my booking site show up on Google?

Each site is built to be easy for Google to read: fast pages, a sitemap, and structured data describing your property, rooms and location. Nobody can promise a ranking. The biggest step you can take is to link your Google Business Profile to your booking site and ask happy guests for Google reviews, which we help with during setup.

## How do deposits, cancellations and refunds work?

By default guests pay a 30% deposit and the balance 7 days before arrival, and you can change this per rate plan. Choose Flexible, Moderate or Strict cancellation, and the policy is shown before payment and in the confirmation. Card and PayPal refunds go back through the provider; bank transfer refunds create a task for your manager.

## What happens to guest data, and is it private?

Guest data is stored in a Singapore data centre, and every guest site links to a privacy notice. Guests can ask for a copy of their data, a correction or deletion, except records the law requires you to keep. Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act brings [key duties for businesses into force on 1 January 2027](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Data-protection-compliance-regime-takes-effect-on-1-Jan-2027/44-795776), and your guest site's privacy notice already refers to it and to the EU GDPR.

## What happens if I miss a subscription payment?

You have a 7-day grace period. After that, your site keeps working but shows "enquire on WhatsApp" instead of instant booking until the payment is made, so guests can still reach you. You can pay your subscription by PayHere, LankaQR or bank transfer. Prices are reviewed each December with 60 days' notice.
