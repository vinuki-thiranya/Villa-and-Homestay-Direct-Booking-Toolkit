For many small properties in Sri Lanka, WhatsApp is the booking office. Guests ask about prices, airport transfers and whether the sea is calm in May, often at midnight their time. How you reply decides whether the conversation becomes a booking or a polite "thanks, we'll think about it". This guide covers the tools in the WhatsApp Business app and the message patterns that work.

## Set up WhatsApp Business properly

If you still use the personal WhatsApp app for the guesthouse, switch to the free WhatsApp Business app. It adds a few tools that save hours in the season.

- **Quick replies.** Save answers to common questions and insert them by typing "/" in a chat. WhatsApp's help centre says you can store up to [50 quick replies](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1791149784551042/?cms_platform=android).
- **Greeting and away messages.** WhatsApp describes these as a way to [greet people the moment they start a conversation and respond even when you're away](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app/).
- **Business profile.** Add your address, a short description, your hours and your website.
- **Click-to-chat link.** A link in the form `wa.me/94XXXXXXXXX` opens a chat with you, and you can [add pre-filled text](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934) such as "Hi, I'd like to check availability". Put it on your website, Google profile and Instagram.

## The shape of a reply that converts

Most enquiries are really one question: "Can I stay with you on these dates, and what will it cost?" A good reply answers that in the first message and gives one clear next step.

1. **Answer the question first.** Dates available or not, and the price.
2. **Add one or two useful details.** What is included, and something specific about the room.
3. **Give one next step.** A booking link or a deposit request, not a list of options.
4. **Set a gentle time limit.** "I can hold this until tomorrow evening" is honest and helps the guest decide.

Long welcome speeches, price lists for every season and "please let us know" endings all slow the booking down.

## Templates you can adapt

These are examples to rewrite in your own voice. Replace the details in square brackets.

### Availability and price

> Hi [Name], thank you for your message. Yes, the garden double is free from [12 to 15 March], 3 nights. The total is [LKR 54,000], including breakfast for two. You can book and pay a 30% deposit here: [link]. I can hold the room until [tomorrow 6 pm] if you need time to decide.

### Not available, but an alternative

> Hi [Name], sorry, we're full on [14 March]. We do have the same room from [15 to 18 March], or our family room on your original dates for [LKR 21,000] a night. Would either of those work?

### The airport transfer question

> Yes, we can arrange a driver from the airport. The drive usually takes about [3 hours] and costs [LKR 18,000] for the car. Send me your flight number once you've booked and I'll confirm the pickup.

### Following up once

> Hi [Name], just checking whether you'd still like the garden double for [12–15 March]. I've had another enquiry for those dates, so let me know by [tomorrow] and I'll keep it for you.

Only say there is another enquiry when there really is one. Guests talk to each other, and a made-up deadline is easy to spot.

### After booking

> Thank you, [Name], your deposit is received and the room is confirmed for [12–15 March]. Here is our location on Google Maps: [link]. Check-in is from [2 pm]. Any questions before you travel, message me here.

## Speed matters more than polish

A guest planning a trip may well be messaging several properties at once. A short, clear reply within minutes often beats a beautiful reply the next morning. Quick replies make this realistic: set up your top 10 answers once and you can reply properly from the beach.

If you cannot reply quickly at certain hours, use an away message that says when you will reply and includes your booking link, so guests who are ready can book straight away.

## Speak the guest's language

Guests from Germany, France and India often write in English, but a short greeting in their own language is noticed. If you get many enquiries from one country, prepare your quick replies in that language too. For Indian guests, payment questions often come up early; our guide to [accepting UPI from Indian guests](/blog/upi-payments-indian-guests) explains how to answer them.

## A note on OTA guests

If the enquiry came through Airbnb, keep the conversation on Airbnb until it is booked. Airbnb's policy says hosts and guests must not [ask each other to communicate off-platform before booking](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2799).
