A direct booking without a deposit is a promise, not a booking. Guests change plans, and an empty room in peak season costs more than any gateway fee. This guide compares the main ways a Sri Lankan villa or guesthouse can take a deposit, and how to set a policy that is fair to both sides.

## Why take a deposit at all

- **It filters out casual bookings.** People who pay something tend to turn up.
- **It covers you when a guest cancels late**, according to the policy you showed them.
- **It gives you working capital** before the season starts.

The aim is not to hold as much money as possible. It is to make the booking real while keeping it easy for the guest.

## The main options compared

Fees and limits change, so check each provider before you sign up. The figures below are from their own pages at the time of writing.

| Method | Best for | Cost to you | Things to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| PayHere | Foreign and local card payments | 2.69%–3.30% by plan, plus 1.0% on foreign currency | Settles in LKR; payment limits on lower plans |
| LankaQR | Sri Lankan guests and Indian guests via UPI | No merchant fee up to LKR 5,000; ask your bank above that | Guest scans a QR with a banking or UPI app |
| PayPal | International guests who prefer PayPal | 4.40% plus a fixed fee for international payments | Receiving in Sri Lanka is new; check your bank |
| Bank transfer | Long stays, larger amounts | Bank charges only | Slower; you must match the slip to the booking |

### PayHere

PayHere is a Sri Lankan gateway that accepts Visa, Mastercard and other cards on a hosted checkout page. Its [fees page](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/) lists three main plans:

- **Lite:** no monthly fee, 3.30% per payment, up to LKR 50,000 per payment and LKR 200,000 a month.
- **Plus:** LKR 3,990 a month, 2.99%, up to LKR 250,000 per payment and LKR 3,000,000 a month.
- **Premium:** LKR 9,990 a month, 2.69%, up to LKR 1,000,000 per payment with no monthly cap.

Foreign-currency payments add 1.0% and are converted and settled in rupees; American Express, Discover and Diners add 0.5%. Pick your plan by your busiest month, not your average one. A Lite account that is fine in June can reach its monthly cap in February.

### LankaQR

LankaQR is Sri Lanka's national QR payment standard: your bank gives you a code, and the guest pays by scanning it with a supported banking or payment app. Since April 2026, the merchant fee on [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 has been waived](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306). Most deposits will be larger than that, so ask your bank what it charges above the threshold.

Indian guests can also pay LankaQR codes with UPI apps. That deserves its own article: [accepting UPI from Indian guests](/blog/upi-payments-indian-guests).

### PayPal

PayPal began letting Sri Lankans [receive international payments through Bank of Ceylon, Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank in May 2026](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Its-official-PayPal-is-here/44-792011). Its Sri Lankan [fee schedule](https://www.paypal.com/lk/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees) lists 4.40% plus a fixed fee (for example USD 0.30) on international payments, and a 3.0% spread when it converts currency. It costs more than PayHere, but some guests from the UK, Europe and Australia will only pay this way.

### Bank transfer

A transfer costs you little, but international transfers can be slow and the guest may pay their own bank's fees. Ask for the booking reference in the transfer description and a photo of the slip, and do not confirm until the money shows in your account.

## Setting a fair deposit policy

There is no single right number. A common pattern, and the default in StayDirect LK, is:

- **30% deposit at booking,** balance due 7 days before arrival;
- **balance at check-in** for flexible rates or short-notice bookings;
- **a larger or full prepayment** for peak dates, long stays or whole-villa bookings.

Tie the refund rules to the deposit, and show them before the guest pays. For example:

- **Flexible:** full refund if cancelled up to 7 days before arrival.
- **Moderate:** 50% refund up to 14 days before arrival.
- **Strict:** deposit not refundable.

Use stricter rules for the dates you could easily resell, and softer ones in the quiet months. Our guide to [pricing for the two monsoons](/blog/monsoon-pricing-south-east-coast) covers which months are which.

## Refunds and records

- **Refund by the same method** the guest paid with wherever possible.
- **Never delete a payment record.** If you refund, record the refund as its own entry, so your books always add up.
- **Send a receipt** for every deposit and balance, with your property name, address and SLTDA number if you are registered.

## Checklist

- [ ] Choose a card gateway and the plan that fits your busiest month.
- [ ] Ask your bank for a LankaQR code and its fee above LKR 5,000.
- [ ] Decide whether you need PayPal for your guest mix.
- [ ] Write your deposit rule: percentage, when the balance is due.
- [ ] Write your cancellation policies and match them to seasons.
- [ ] Run a small test payment through each method before the season.
- [ ] Set a routine for matching bank-transfer slips to bookings.
