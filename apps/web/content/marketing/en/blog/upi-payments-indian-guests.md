Indian travellers are the largest group of visitors to Sri Lanka, and UPI is how a great many people in India pay for everyday things. If your guesthouse can take a UPI payment, you remove one of the main reasons an Indian family books through an OTA instead of directly with you. This guide explains how UPI works in Sri Lanka and how to set it up for deposits and balances.

## Why it matters

In 2025, about [531,000 Indian visitors came to Sri Lanka, 22% of the 2.36 million international arrivals](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837) and the largest single source market. A guest who can pay you the way they pay at home has one less reason to book through an app.

## How UPI works in Sri Lanka

UPI is India's real-time payment system. Since [February 2024](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=97199), it has been accepted in Sri Lanka through a link between India's NPCI International and LankaPay, the operator of the national payment network.

The important part for you: Indian guests pay by scanning a **LankaQR** code. LankaPay describes it as acceptance of [any UPI or BHIM-enabled app at LankaQR merchant locations](https://lankapay.net/en/npci-international). In March 2026, NPCI International said merchants who already have LankaQR [only need to switch on UPI acceptance](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837).

So the steps are:

1. You get a LankaQR code from your bank (or confirm the one you have).
2. You ask your bank to make sure UPI acceptance is enabled on it.
3. The guest scans the code with their UPI app and pays.
4. The money arrives in your bank account.

## Questions to ask your bank

Banks handle the details differently, so ask before the season:

- **Is UPI enabled on my LankaQR code?** If not, what do I need to do?
- **What is the fee?** The merchant fee on [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 was waived in April 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306), but most deposits are larger. Ask what applies to a UPI payment above that.
- **What arrives in my account, and when?** Ask how the payment is settled and how quickly it shows.
- **How will I be notified?** An SMS or app alert for each payment makes checking much easier.
- **Can a guest pay before arriving in Sri Lanka?** Some guests will want to pay a deposit from home by scanning a QR image you send. Ask your bank, and test it with someone in India before you promise it.

## What the guest needs to do

Indian UPI apps such as PhonePe, Google Pay and BHIM have a [UPI International setting](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/how-to-activate-upi-international-on-phonepe-google-pay-and-bhim-a-step-by-step-guide-2024-03-28-923535) that guests may need to switch on, with their UPI PIN, before paying abroad. Tell guests this in your booking confirmation, so they are not sorting it out at your reception desk late at night.

Guests' banks and apps also set their own limits on international payments. If a guest's payment is refused, the simplest fallback is to split it into two payments or to pay the balance by card.

## A simple payment routine

Most problems with QR payments are about matching money to bookings. A routine helps:

1. **Quote the amount in rupees.** Show the exact LKR amount, and an approximate figure in Indian rupees for reference.
2. **Give a booking reference.** Ask the guest to add it to the payment note if their app allows.
3. **Ask for a screenshot.** A screenshot of the successful payment helps you find it.
4. **Confirm only when the money shows.** Check your bank alert or app, then confirm the booking.
5. **Send a receipt** with the amount, date and what is still due.

## Make Indian guests feel expected

Payment is one part of a good direct booking. A few small things help families from India choose you:

- **Reply in Hindi or English,** whichever the guest uses. Our guide to [WhatsApp messages that turn enquiries into bookings](/blog/whatsapp-messages-that-convert) has templates you can adapt.
- **Mention vegetarian breakfast options** and family rooms clearly, if you offer them.
- **Show prices in Indian rupees** on your booking site as a guide, while charging in LKR.
- **Explain the deposit and the balance** in one short message, with the QR attached.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm with your bank that UPI is enabled on your LankaQR code.
- [ ] Ask about fees above LKR 5,000 and how payments are settled.
- [ ] Turn on payment alerts for your account.
- [ ] Test a payment from a UPI app, including from India if you want pre-arrival deposits.
- [ ] Add a "switch on UPI International" note to your confirmation message.
- [ ] Keep a card or bank-transfer fallback for larger amounts.
- [ ] Prepare Hindi and English replies for common questions.
