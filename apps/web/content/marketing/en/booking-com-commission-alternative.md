You do not need to leave Booking.com or Airbnb to pay them less. They are good at putting your property in front of people who have never heard of you. They add little for the guest who stayed last year, the friend that guest sends you, or the couple who found you on Google Maps. Those guests can book on your own site.

StayDirect gives your villa, guesthouse, homestay or surf camp its own booking site, keeps your calendars in sync with the OTAs, and sends deposits to your own account.

## What an OTA booking costs you

**Booking.com** does not publish one rate. Its own help pages say the commission percentage [varies by country, property type and location](https://partner.booking.com/en-us/help/commission-invoices-tax/commission/understanding-our-commission), that your exact rate is in your agreement, and that programmes such as Genius, Preferred Partner or Visibility Booster can raise the percentage on a booking. Look at a recent reservation statement to see what you actually paid.

**Airbnb** now charges most hosts a single host-only fee. Its help centre says [most hosts pay 15.5%](https://www.airbnb.co.in/help/article/1857), and that this structure is mandatory for most hosts, including traditional hospitality listings.

## What a direct booking costs you

A direct booking still has costs, and you should count them honestly:

- **Card payments** through PayHere cost [3.30% on Lite (no monthly fee), 2.99% on Plus (LKR 3,990 a month) or 2.69% on Premium (LKR 9,990 a month), plus 1.0% when the guest pays in a foreign currency](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/).
- **LankaQR** payments, including UPI from Indian guests, carry [no merchant fee up to LKR 5,000](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306). Above that, ask your bank for its rate.
- **PayPal** has been able to [pay international money into Sri Lankan bank accounts since May 2026](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Its-official-PayPal-is-here/44-792011), through Bank of Ceylon, Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank to begin with. PayPal sets its own fees.
- **Bank transfer** and **cash at check-in** cost only your bank's charges.
- **StayDirect** itself, on a flat or percentage plan (below).

The money never passes through us. Every payment method is connected to your own merchant or bank account.

## Keep the OTAs for discovery, move the rest direct

The aim is not to take your property off Booking.com. It is to stop paying commission on guests who would have found you anyway.

1. **Stay listed, stay in sync.** StayDirect imports your Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and Vrbo calendars every 15 minutes and exports your direct bookings back to them, so a room sold on your site is blocked everywhere. Read [iCal sync and double bookings](/blog/ical-sync-double-bookings).
2. **Ask in person.** A card at reception that says "book direct next time" with a QR code, and a word at check-out, is the simplest way to win a repeat guest. Do not use contact details from an OTA booking for marketing: an industry analysis of Booking.com's terms notes that they bar using guest contact details to [send unsolicited electronic messages](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4134098/the-email-you-want-is-the-one-she-gives-you-for-a-reason). Check your own agreement, and ask guests directly if they would like to hear from you.
3. **Be findable by name.** Point your Google Business Profile at your own site. See [Google Business Profile for guesthouses](/blog/google-business-profile-guesthouse).
4. **Give a reason, not just a lower price.** A free airport pickup or a smaller deposit is simple to offer. If you want to show a lower price on your own site, read the rate rules in your OTA agreement first.
5. **Remember your direct guests.** On Pro and above, the guest CRM sends past direct guests a "book direct next time" code 11 months after their stay, in time for the same season next year. See [moving repeat guests direct](/blog/move-repeat-guests-direct).

## Questions owners ask

**"Will I lose my Booking.com guests?"** No. You stay listed and keep taking new guests there. Only repeat and referral guests change where they book.

**"Guests trust Booking.com more than a small website."** Show your reviews, a clear cancellation policy before payment, and a hosted PayHere or PayPal checkout. See [getting more reviews](/blog/getting-more-reviews).

**"What about double bookings?"** StayDirect refuses a second booking of the same room and night. OTAs read your calendar on their own schedule, so if two channels do clash, you get a WhatsApp alert with both booking references.

**"I do not have time."** Setup takes about an hour at your property.

**"Card fees will eat the saving."** In the example, even with every guest on a foreign card, fees are a fraction of the commission. LankaQR and cash cost less again.

**"What if I have a bad month?"** Use the percentage plan: no direct bookings, no fee.
