We will replace it with figures from a real pilot property, with the owner's permission, after their first full season.

## The property

Villa Lagoon is an imaginary 5-room villa near the lagoon in Bentota, on the south-west coast. It is family-run: the owner handles rates and guests, a manager handles check-ins, and two housekeepers clean. Rooms sell at an illustrative LKR 22,000 a night.

Like the rest of the south-west coast, Bentota's busy months fall outside the south-west monsoon, which the Department of Meteorology dates [May to September](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf). The example below looks at a December–March season.

## The problem

Before StayDirect, in this example:

- Almost every booking came through Booking.com and Airbnb, including guests who had stayed before and friends they had sent.
- The owner updated both calendars by hand, and twice in a season a room was sold on both.
- The manager answered the same WhatsApp questions about directions, airport transfers and breakfast many times a day.
- Guests who wanted to book direct had no way to pay a deposit except a bank transfer, which took days to confirm.

## Setup

Setup took one visit of about an hour:

1. Rooms, rates, photos and house rules entered; a December–March seasonal rate and a 3-night minimum stay added.
2. Each room's StayDirect calendar link pasted into Airbnb and Booking.com, and theirs into StayDirect; first sync checked.
3. PayHere Plus connected for card deposits, LankaQR code added for Indian guests, PayPal added for guests who prefer it.
4. WhatsApp number connected; quick replies loaded in English and German, with directions and a map pin.
5. Google Business Profile updated with the new booking link; a "book direct next time" card printed for reception.

The site went live at villa-lagoon.staydirect.lk the same day, on the Basic plan.

## First season results (illustrative)

**All figures below are made up for illustration.** They use the same assumptions as our pricing examples: Booking.com commission assumed at 15% (each owner's rate is in their own agreement), every direct guest paying the full stay by foreign card through PayHere Plus at 2.99% plus 1.0% for foreign currency, as listed on [PayHere's fee page](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/).

| Measure, December–March | Illustrative figure |
|---|---|
| Room-nights available (5 rooms × 121 nights) | 605 |
| Room-nights sold (70% occupancy) | 424 |
| Room-nights booked direct (25%) | 106 |
| Value of direct bookings | LKR 2,332,000 |
| Commission avoided at an assumed 15% | LKR 349,800 |
| PayHere Plus card fees (3.99%) | −LKR 93,047 |
| PayHere Plus monthly fee (4 months × 3,990) | −LKR 15,960 |
| StayDirect Basic flat fee (4 months × 5,000) | −LKR 20,000 |
| **Kept in this example** | **LKR 220,793** |
| Double bookings | 0 (illustrative) |

On the percentage plan, the StayDirect line would have been 2.5% of LKR 2,332,000, or LKR 58,300, so at this volume the flat plan was the cheaper choice. Monthly direct bookings of about LKR 583,000 are also above PayHere Lite's LKR 200,000 monthly limit, which is why the example uses Plus.

What a real case study will add, measured from the owner's StayDirect reports:

- direct share of room-nights, month by month, against the season before;
- where direct guests came from: repeat guests, referrals, Google, social media;
- deposit methods used (card, PayPal, LankaQR, bank transfer, cash);
- calendar clashes caught and how they were resolved;
- hours of WhatsApp work saved, as estimated by the owner.
