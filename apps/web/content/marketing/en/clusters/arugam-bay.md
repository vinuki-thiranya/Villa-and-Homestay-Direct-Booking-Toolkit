Arugam Bay has one of the sharpest seasons in Sri Lanka. For about half the year the surf fills the town; for much of the rest it is quiet. Software that bills you the same every month does not fit that pattern. StayDirect gives your surf guesthouse its own booking site, syncs your Airbnb and Booking.com calendars, and lets you pay a percentage of direct bookings instead of a fixed fee.

## Season, shoulder and monsoon

The Surf Atlas puts [Arugam Bay's best surf between April and September](https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/arugam-bay-surf/), says the town gets packed around August, and describes October and November as a decent time with smaller crowds and lower hotel prices. December to March is the low season, when the water gets murky and most breaks suffer.

That is the reverse of the south coast. The Department of Meteorology notes that [eastern and northern regions receive their rain during the north-east monsoon](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf), which it dates December to February. Climate data for nearby Pottuvil shows its [wettest months as November, December and January](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka).

So you have three seasons to price, not two: a full season, a shoulder with cheaper rooms, and a wet low season when you may close some rooms or the whole property.

## Who books, and what that means for payments

Your core guests are surfers, and the Surf Atlas notes that beginners and beach visitors have joined them in recent years. Nationally, India was the largest source market in January–June 2026, followed by the UK and Russia, according to [SLTDA figures reported by India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=220356).

Surfers who return each season are your best direct-booking guests. They know the place, they know you, and they do not need an OTA to find you again. Give them a reason to book on your own site, such as a free board-storage rack or an airport transfer at cost, and send them your link when next season's rates open.

For payments:

- **Cards** through PayHere cost [2.99% on Plus (LKR 3,990 a month) or 3.30% on Lite, plus 1.0% for foreign-currency payments](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/).
- **PayPal** can now be used by Sri Lankan businesses to [receive international payments through local banks](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Its-official-PayPal-is-here/44-792011), which suits guests who prefer not to enter a card.
- **UPI** from Indian guests works by [scanning a LankaQR code](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837).

Your WhatsApp quick replies can cover surf conditions, board hire, tuk-tuk prices and the airport transfer, in English, German, French, Hindi or Sinhala, or in any language you write yourself.

## Tips for Arugam Bay owners

1. **Open next season's dates before you close.** Load April–September rates while your last guests are still with you, and send returning surfers the link. Read [moving repeat guests direct](/blog/move-repeat-guests-direct).
2. **Block closed months on every channel.** If you close for part of the low season, block those dates once in StayDirect and the block flows to Airbnb, Booking.com and Agoda through iCal. See [iCal sync and double bookings](/blog/ical-sync-double-bookings).
3. **Price the shoulder separately.** October and November can still bring surfers looking for quieter waves and lower prices. See [monsoon pricing on the south and east coasts](/blog/monsoon-pricing-south-east-coast).
4. **Take a deposit for August.** In the busiest weeks a no-show costs you a full-price room. Use a non-refundable deposit on peak dates. Read [deposits with PayHere and LankaQR](/blog/deposits-payhere-lankaqr).
5. **Offer long stays.** If some of your surfers stay for weeks, a monthly rate keeps them on your own site rather than an OTA. See [digital nomad long stays](/blog/digital-nomad-long-stays).
