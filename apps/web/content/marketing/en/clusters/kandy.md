Kandy is not a one-season town. Guests visit the Temple of the Tooth all year, and once a year the Esala Perahera brings ten nights of processions through the city. Pricing those nights well, and keeping the rest of the year steady, is where your own booking site earns its keep.

## The year in Kandy

Climate data for Kandy shows rain [at its lowest from January to March and its highest from October to December](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka), with October the wettest month. The Department of Meteorology's [second inter-monsoon runs from October to November and the north-east monsoon from December to February](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf); during the inter-monsoons, afternoon thunder showers fall over most of the island.

For an owner, the weather matters less here than on the coast. What matters more is the calendar of events. The Sacred City of Kandy is a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/450/). In 2026 the Perahera ran [from 18 to 28 August](https://blog.wego.com/kandy-esala-perahera/), with five Kumbal nights, five Randoli nights and a daytime procession on the last day. The dates follow the lunar calendar, so they move each year.

## Who books, and what that means for you

Kandy sits between the coast, the cultural triangle and the tea country, so a guest arriving mid-route, with a driver or by train, needs clear directions before anything else. Nationally, India was the largest source market in the first half of 2026, followed by the UK and Russia, according to [SLTDA figures reported by India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=220356). If Indian families make up part of your guest list, a Hindi confirmation and an easy UPI payment remove two reasons to book through an OTA instead.

- **UPI**: Indian guests can [scan your LankaQR code with a UPI app](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837), and [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 carry no merchant fee](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306).
- **Cards**: PayHere charges [2.99% on Plus or 3.30% on Lite, plus 1.0% when the guest pays in a foreign currency](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/). Local guests paying in rupees avoid the foreign-currency fee.
- **Bank transfer**: the guest uploads a slip and your manager confirms it from the app.

Confirmations go by email and WhatsApp in English, German, French, Hindi or Sinhala, with your directions and a map pin that the guest can forward to their driver.

## Tips for Kandy owners

1. **Load Perahera rates as soon as dates are announced.** Add them as a seasonal rate with a minimum stay, so the best nights are not sold one at a time at a normal price.
2. **Use a firm policy for festival nights.** Attach a non-refundable deposit to the Perahera rate plan and keep your flexible policy for the rest of the year. See [deposits with PayHere and LankaQR](/blog/deposits-payhere-lankaqr).
3. **Write to last year's festival guests first.** Guests who stayed for the Perahera may want to come back. Send them your direct link before the dates go on the OTAs. Read [moving repeat guests direct](/blog/move-repeat-guests-direct).
4. **Let your staff work in Sinhala.** The owner app and owner web run in Sinhala as well as English, so a manager or cleaner can use it in the language they read best.
5. **Check your SLTDA registration is current.** SLTDA says guest houses and home stay units [should register under section 48 of the Tourism Act No. 38 of 2005](https://sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/ARE_YOU_A%20SERVICE_PROVIDER_Paper_Advertisment_2020_english2899360352.pdf). StayDirect prints your SLTDA number on invoices. See [SLTDA registration basics](/blog/sltda-registration-basics).
