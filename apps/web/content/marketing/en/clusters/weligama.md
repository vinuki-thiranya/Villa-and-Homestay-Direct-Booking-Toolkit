Weligama sells something most towns cannot: a first surf lesson. Your guests are often booking a room, a board and an instructor together, and an OTA listing only sells the room. StayDirect gives your surf camp or guesthouse its own booking site, lets guests add lessons and transfers when they book, and keeps your Airbnb and Booking.com calendars in step.

## The surf season sets the calendar

The Surf Atlas calls Weligama ["the undisputed beginner hub of Sri Lanka"](https://thesurfatlas.com/sri-lanka-surf/weligama-surf/), with the widest choice of surf schools anywhere on the island bar Arugam Bay. The same guide puts the best surfing in the south's dry season, roughly November to March, and notes that during the monsoon most surfers head east to Arugam Bay.

The Department of Meteorology describes the south-west monsoon, [from May to September](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf), as the season when the weather is concentrated on the south-west of the island and the hills. On this coast, [June to August is rainy and the sea is rough](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka).

So a Weligama surf camp has a clear high season and a long, quiet middle of the year. That shapes how you should pay for software as much as how you price rooms.

## Who books, and what they need from you

Beginners want to know three things before they pay: is there a lesson, is the board included, and how far is the walk to the break. Put those answers on your booking site and in your WhatsApp quick replies, and you will spend far less time typing.

Across the country, India was the largest source market in the first half of 2026, with the UK second and Russia third, according to [SLTDA figures reported by India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=220356). Guest sites and confirmations in StayDirect run in English, German, French, Hindi and Sinhala, and you can write your own quick replies in any other language your guests use.

Payments follow the guest:

- **Card guests** pay through PayHere, which charges [2.99% on the Plus plan (LKR 3,990 a month) or 3.30% on Lite, plus 1.0% for foreign-currency payments](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/).
- **Indian guests** can pay with a UPI app by [scanning LankaQR](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837); [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 carry no merchant fee](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306), so small deposits and add-ons under that amount cost you nothing to collect.
- **Lessons and transfers** can be sold as add-ons. The surf school or driver gets most of the price, you keep 10–15% and StayDirect takes 10%.

## Tips for Weligama owners

1. **Sell the room and the lesson together.** List your surf school's lessons and board hire as add-ons, so a beginner can book everything in one go and pay one deposit.
2. **Use the percentage plan if your monsoon months are empty.** On 2.5% of direct bookings, a month with no direct bookings costs you nothing.
3. **Keep the quiet months working.** Monthly rates and a Wi-Fi speed badge can fill rooms with remote workers when the surfers leave. Read [long stays for digital nomads](/blog/digital-nomad-long-stays).
4. **Answer the same five questions once.** Board, lesson times, distance to the break, breakfast, airport transfer. Our [WhatsApp messages that convert](/blog/whatsapp-messages-that-convert) guide has templates.
5. **Photograph what beginners worry about,** such as the board rack, the walk to the beach and the room, not just the sunset. See [photos that sell rooms](/blog/photos-that-sell-rooms).
