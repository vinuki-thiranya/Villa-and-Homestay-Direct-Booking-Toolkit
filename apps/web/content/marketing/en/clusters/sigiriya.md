A stay near Sigiriya is usually part of a bigger plan: the rock, the old cities nearby and perhaps a jeep safari to see elephants. A guesthouse or villa that can book the room and the safari together, and answer the "what time should we leave?" questions on WhatsApp, gives guests a reason to book with you rather than through an OTA.

## Weather in the dry zone

Sigiriya lies in the northern part of Matale District, and the [Sigiriya Sanctuary is dry zone forest](https://lakpura.ca/pages/sigiriya-sanctuary). Here the pattern is close to the reverse of the south-west coast. The Department of Meteorology notes that [eastern and northern regions receive their rain during the north-east monsoon](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf). Climate data for Anuradhapura, the nearest station shown in [this climate summary](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka), has its rain at a maximum between October and December and a minimum from June to August.

That means your driest months fall in the middle of the south coast's monsoon. Set your rates from your own dry and wet months, not the coast's.

The [Ancient City of Sigiriya](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/202/) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. One seasonal draw is worth planning around: every year, [August through September, up to 300 Asian elephants gather at Minneriya Lake](https://rove.me/to/sri-lanka/elephant-gathering).

## Who books, and how they pay

Guests touring the island with a driver need a pin and directions the driver can follow, and a check-in time that fits a long day on the road. Nationally, India was the largest source market in January–June 2026, followed by the UK and Russia, according to [SLTDA figures reported by India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=220356). If you also host Sri Lankan families, remember that they pay differently from foreign guests.

- **Sri Lankan guests** can pay through PayHere with local cards, wallets or internet banking at [2.99% on Plus or 3.30% on Lite](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/), with no foreign-currency fee because they pay in rupees.
- **Foreign card guests** pay the same rate plus 1.0% for foreign-currency payments.
- **Indian guests** can [scan your LankaQR code with a UPI app](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837), with no merchant fee on [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306).

Confirmations go in English, German, French, Hindi or Sinhala, with a map pin your guest can forward to their driver.

## Tips for Sigiriya owners

1. **Sell the safari with the room.** Add your trusted jeep driver as an add-on. SLTDA lists [vehicle safari tour operators among the services that should register](https://sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/ARE_YOU_A%20SERVICE_PROVIDER_Paper_Advertisment_2020_english2899360352.pdf), so choose registered providers. The driver gets most of the price, you keep 10–15% and StayDirect takes 10%.
2. **Price July to September as a season of its own.** Your dry months and the Minneriya gathering fall when the south coast is wet. See [monsoon pricing](/blog/monsoon-pricing-south-east-coast).
3. **Write for the driver as well as the guest.** Put a clear map pin and landmark directions in your confirmation and quick replies. See [WhatsApp messages that convert](/blog/whatsapp-messages-that-convert).
4. **Sync before you list on a new OTA.** If you add Agoda or Vrbo, paste their calendar link into StayDirect on day one. See [iCal sync and double bookings](/blog/ical-sync-double-bookings).
5. **Keep your Google listing pointing to your own site,** so a guest who finds you on the map can book you directly. See [Google Business Profile for guesthouses](/blog/google-business-profile-guesthouse).
