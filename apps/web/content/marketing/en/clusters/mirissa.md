Mirissa earns most of its year in a few calm months, so commission and double bookings in those months hurt most. StayDirect gives your guesthouse or villa its own booking site, keeps Booking.com and Airbnb in step with it, and sends deposits straight to your own account.

## A year in two halves

Sri Lanka's Department of Meteorology divides the year into four seasons, and during the south-west monsoon, from May to September, the weather is ["confined mainly to the southwest quarter and hill country"](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/Activities/qmws_2010/Presentation/S404_Dayananda.pdf). On the south-west coast from Colombo to Tangalle, [June to August is rainy and the sea is rough](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka). The same climate data shows nearby Galle's wettest months as October, May and November, so the October–November inter-monsoon can be wet as well.

The calm months line up with whale watching. The best time to see whales and dolphins off Galle and Mirissa is [late November to mid-April, with blue whales most likely between February and mid-March](https://www.responsibletravel.com/holidays/sri-lanka/travel-guide/blue-whale-watching-in-sri-lanka).

What this means for you: set your high-season rates, minimum stays and cancellation policy before November, and treat the monsoon months as a separate business with their own prices. Our guide to [monsoon pricing on the south and east coasts](/blog/monsoon-pricing-south-east-coast) goes through this in detail.

## Who books, and how they pay

Across Sri Lanka, India was the largest source market in January–June 2026 with about 26% of arrivals, followed by the UK and Russia, according to [SLTDA figures reported by India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=220356). Your own guest list may look different, so check the "revenue by channel" and guest reports in StayDirect after your first season.

Each group pays differently, and your booking site should offer each the method they already use:

- **European, UK and Australian guests** usually pay by card. PayHere charges [3.30% on its Lite plan with no monthly fee, or 2.99% on Plus at LKR 3,990 a month, plus 1.0% when a guest pays in a foreign currency](https://www.payhere.lk/fees/), settled to you in rupees. Lite is capped at LKR 50,000 per payment and LKR 200,000 a month, which a busy February can pass.
- **PayPal** now lets Sri Lankan businesses [receive international payments through Bank of Ceylon, Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Its-official-PayPal-is-here/44-792011), since May 2026.
- **Indian guests** can [scan a LankaQR code with a UPI app](https://www.ft.lk/travel-tourism/NPCI-International-eyes-Indian-tourists-to-boost-UPI-merchant-acceptance-in-Sri-Lanka/27-789837), and the merchant fee on [LankaQR payments up to LKR 5,000 has been waived since April 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lanka-launches-national-QR-adoption-drive-scraps-merchant-fees-for-transactions-under-Rs-5-000/273-337306). See [UPI payments for Indian guests](/blog/upi-payments-indian-guests).

Confirmations go out by email and WhatsApp with your directions and Google Maps pin, in English, German, French, Hindi or Sinhala, so you are not typing the same "how do I find you from the bus stand?" answer every evening.

## Example: what 20 direct nights are worth in January

This is an illustration, not a promise. Change the numbers to your own.

**Assumptions:** a 4-room guesthouse on the Basic plan; LKR 18,000 a night; 20 room-nights in January that came direct from repeat or referral guests instead of through Booking.com. Booking.com sets your commission in your own agreement and it [varies by country, property type and location](https://partner.booking.com/en-us/help/commission-invoices-tax/commission/understanding-our-commission); we assume 15%. Every guest pays the full stay by foreign card through PayHere Plus, which overstates the card fees if some pay the balance in cash.

## Tips for Mirissa owners

1. **Choose your PayHere plan by your best month, not your average one.** If peak-season deposits will pass LKR 200,000 a month, start on Plus so a payment is not refused in February.
2. **Use a stricter policy for February and March.** StayDirect lets you attach a cancellation policy and deposit to each rate plan, so peak dates can carry a non-refundable deposit while monsoon dates stay flexible. Read [deposits with PayHere and LankaQR](/blog/deposits-payhere-lankaqr).
3. **Offer the whale trip properly.** SLTDA lists ["Viewing of Whales and Dolphins"](https://sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/ARE_YOU_A%20SERVICE_PROVIDER_Paper_Advertisment_2020_english2899360352.pdf) among the tourism services that should register, so add only registered operators as add-ons on your site.
4. **Ask happy guests to book direct next time.** A card at reception and a message after check-out do more than any advert. See [moving repeat guests direct](/blog/move-repeat-guests-direct).
5. **Link your Google Business Profile to your own site,** so people who search your name land on your booking page. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-guesthouse) shows how.
